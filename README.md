## 📂 Top Open-Source Repositories

Dive into our public Python repositories to run your own trading bots, share signals privately, or backtest custom strategies:

### 1. [`cryptotrademate-trading-bot`](https://github.com/Cryptotrademate/cryptotrademate-trading-bot)
> **Free, Open-Source Automated Crypto Trading Bot (Grid & DCA)**
* **Key Features:** Supports automated Grid and Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) strategies out of the box.
* **Integrations:** Connects directly to major exchanges including **Binance** and **Coinbase** via API using Python and `CCXT`.
* **Portfolio & Control:** Tracks live portfolio balances in USDT and allows you to monitor and execute trades straight from **Telegram**.

### 2. [`cryptotrademate-decentralized-crypto-signal-sharing-bot`](https://github.com/Cryptotrademate/cryptotrademate-decentralized-crypto-signal-sharing-bot)
> **Self-Hosted, Privacy-First Crypto Signal Sharing Engine**
* **Key Features:** Share your own trading signals or subscribe to custom strategy feeds without relying on centralized third-party intermediaries.
* **Customization & Alerts:** Configure custom trading pairs, track historical signal accuracy, and automate instant notifications via **Telegram** or the built-in **Web UI**.

### 3. [`cryptotrademate-backtesting-tool`](https://github.com/Cryptotrademate/cryptotrademate-backtesting-tool)
> **Python Strategy Simulation & Optimization Suite**
* **Key Features:** Simulate, analyze, and fine-tune crypto trading strategies against historical market data before risking live capital.
* **Built For:** Quantitative analysts, Python developers, and algorithmic traders looking to validate performance metrics and API integrations.
